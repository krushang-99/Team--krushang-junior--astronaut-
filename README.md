# Team Krushang - Junior Astronaut Trainer
### NASA Space Apps 2026 | Challenge: ISS - Abandoned but Not Forgotten
**Goal Award: Best Use of Storytelling | From: Pune, India**

#### Our Story - By Krushang (9 yrs)
Ek chhota astronaut Krushang training kar raha hai. Use pata chalta hai ki hamara ISS 25 saal se space me hai aur ab use retire karna padega. Ab Krushang ko bada decision lena hai - ISS pe kaun se 3 experiments ko bachana hai jo future ke bachchon ke kaam ayenge? Ye project bachchon ko sikhata hai ki space mission me choice karni padti hai.

#### What We Made
1. Team Logo - Junior Astronaut with ISS
2. Story Poster - Why ISS is important  
3. Game Idea - Choose 3 to Save (card game for kids)
4. Video - 1 min video explanation (coming soon)

#### NASA Data Used
- NASA ISS Facts and ISS Retirement Info
